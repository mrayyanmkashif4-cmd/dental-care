PATIENT EDUCATION

Know Your Oral Health

A healthy mouth, free of infection and injury, is important for your overall health. Learn about common dental problems, how to prevent them, and when to see a dentist.

TEETH
Tooth Decay (Cavities)

Bacteria turn sugar into acid that slowly damages enamel and creates holes in the tooth. Early treatment is simple; late treatment is not.

  • Toothache or pain when biting
  • Brown or black spots on teeth
  • Limit sugary snacks and drinks
TEETH
Tooth Sensitivity

Worn enamel or exposed roots can make teeth react sharply to hot, cold, sweet or acidic food and drinks.

  • Short, sharp pain with cold or hot
  • Discomfort while brushing
  • Use a soft brush and desensitising toothpaste
GUMS
Gum Disease

Plaque left on the gum line causes inflammation (gingivitis). If untreated it can damage the bone that holds your teeth.

  • Red, swollen or bleeding gums
  • Gums pulling away from teeth
  • Floss daily and get regular cleanings
MOUTH
Bad Breath

Most often caused by bacteria on the tongue and between teeth, but it can also point to gum disease, dry mouth or other health issues.

  • Persistent unpleasant taste or odour
  • Dry mouth, coated tongue
  • Clean your tongue and drink plenty of water
MOUTH
Mouth Ulcers

Small painful sores inside the mouth are usually harmless and heal on their own, but should be checked if they last long.

  • Painful white or yellow sores
  • Burning before the sore appears
  • See a dentist if it lasts over 3 weeks
KIDS
Children's Dental Care

Baby teeth guide adult teeth into place, so looking after them matters. Healthy habits learned early last a lifetime.

  • First dental visit by age one
  • Supervise brushing until about age 7
  • Avoid sleeping with a sugary bottle
DAILY ROUTINE

4 Habits For Healthy Teeth

1
Brush Twice A Day

Two minutes with a fluoride toothpaste and a soft brush.

2
Floss Daily

Clean between teeth where the brush cannot reach.

3
Eat Smart

Cut down on sugary snacks, sweets and fizzy drinks.

4
Visit Regularly

A checkup and cleaning every six months.

Do
  • Replace your toothbrush every 3 months
  • Drink water after meals
  • Wear a mouthguard for contact sports
  • Use fluoride toothpaste
Don't
  • Smoke or chew tobacco
  • Brush too hard
  • Use teeth to open bottles or packets
  • Ignore a toothache
See A Dentist Quickly If
  • Swelling of the face or gums
  • Severe pain that does not settle
  • A tooth is knocked out or broken
  • Bleeding that will not stop
